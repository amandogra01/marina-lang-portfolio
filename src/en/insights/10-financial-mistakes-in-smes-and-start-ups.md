Many of the challenges growing companies face in finance repeat themselves. The following
ten points are among the most common causes of problems in accounting and controlling.

1. **No liquidity planning** — revenue growth gets mistaken for cash solvency.
2. **No clear cost center structure** — costs can't be allocated on a causal basis.
3. **Manual, error-prone processes** — especially around closing and invoicing.
4. **Controlling structures built too late** — KPIs are missing exactly when decisions
   need to be made.
5. **Unclear provisioning** — risks get recognized too late or incompletely.
6. **No separation between accounting and controlling** — operational and strategic
   perspectives blur together.
7. **Underestimated effort for ERP rollouts** — systems get implemented without sufficient
   business preparation.
8. **No regular actual-vs-plan comparisons** — variances are caught too late.
9. **Compliance treated as secondary** — until an audit is imminent.
10. **Missing external expertise during growth phases** — internal capacity often can't
    keep up with increasing complexity.

## Conclusion

Most of these mistakes are avoidable once accounting and controlling are professionalized
early — ideally before growth outpaces the structures already in place.
